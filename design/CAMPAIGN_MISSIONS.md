# SEVENFALL: THE WAR FOR CREATION
## Campaign mission atlas 1.0: 75 missions, three perspectives

**Status:** approved-design candidate / narrative and mission specification only. **No campaign missions are currently implemented or playable.** This atlas expands the [story bible](STORY_BIBLE.md), preserves the original eight mission titles per faction in their narrative order, and supplies **25 human, 25 angel and 25 demon** missions.

> The Seventh Seal broke. The world did not end. Three armies arrived to decide what came next. Only one of them had been asked to live there.

## Campaign contract

- The campaigns are **Ashes of Earth (Humans)**, **War in Heaven (Angels)** and **The Ninth Mouth (Demons)**. Each has **five acts of five missions**, 25 missions in total, with a different perspective on the same historical crisis.
- **One campaign's mission index is not the same date as another's.** Crossover events are intentionally depicted out of sequence as characters obtain new information; the continuity anchors below pin down the shared outcomes.
- The endings (H25, A25 and D25) are mutually exclusive **alternative timelines** after the same siege of the Harrow of Dawn. Six final ethical outcomes exist, two per parent campaign. Nothing requires a canonical winner.
- Each mission has a primary gameplay goal, an optional objective, a failure condition and a story consequence. Optional objectives provide new choices, intelligence, allied support or changed scenes without converting the resource economy into capture-point requisition.
- Where a mission needs new civilian actors, creatures, cinematic powers, naval units, unique terrain, custom scripting or progression flags, those are **future features**. Only actors in the repository's existing OpenRA rules are to be considered already defined.
- Most operations start with a construction yard or MCV, then use **power → physical Veilglass gathering → refinery unloading → unit production → combat**. Some tightly scoped convoy, infiltration and defensive missions have fixed assets or a reduced tech tree, but are not replacements for the main classic C&C loop.

### Five acts of the apocalypse

| Act | Mission range | Narrative movement | Gameplay escalation |
|---|---|---|---|
| I. The Breaking | 01–05 | First descent, first incursions, survival and the discovery of Veilglass | Deploy, power, harvest, infantry, vehicles |
| II. The War of Claims | 06–10 | Outposts, contested territories, coerced evacuation and military expansion | Harvester ambushes, siege fronts, branch-exclusive units |
| III. The False Edicts | 11–15 | Propaganda, captivity, forged authority and three internal faction crises | Counter-intelligence, terrain hazards, multi-front defense |
| IV. The Witness Ledger | 16–20 | The ancient covenant is reconstructed and Vhal's conspiracy exposed | Escort, archive assaults, coalition choices, advanced infrastructure |
| V. The Harrow of Dawn | 21–25 | Three armies mobilize for the final Seamfall and fight over the right to rule | Large-scale economy, multi-stage assaults, alternative superweapon endings |

### Repeated map families
**Broken Choir** is the existing proving-ground location and an important in-story battlefield, not a claim that the flat current test map already has campaign events. **The Glass Fields**, **Ashen Causeway**, **Sable Crown**, **Salt Meridian**, **Empty Gate** and **Harrow of Dawn** are planned theatres. Other maps are districts or tactical regions within those theatres.

## CAMPAIGN I: ASHES OF EARTH (HUMANS)

**Player role:** field commander of the Last Dominion, aligned with Bastion Directorate, Free Earth Union or Ashen Covenant. **Human protagonists:** Marshal Mara Voss, Engineer-General Idris Awan, Doctor-Archivist Elian Vey and witness-librarian Tamar Rell. **Moral arc:** survival → military independence → control versus genuine consent.

### ACT I: THE BREAKING (H01–H05)

#### H01. The Day the Sky Broke
**Theatre:** Crown Vale, Ashen Causeway. **Primary:** escort three refugee groups to shelter, use starting troops to clear an infernal scouting incursion and deploy the emergency MCV. **Optional:** save a stranded field-hospital convoy. **Failure:** convoy quorum lost or every shelter falls. **Story:** the gold-lit invaders initially appear to be rescuers, but quarantine orders follow without asking the survivors.

#### H02. Last Light
**Theatre:** Shelter Delta. **Primary:** power hospitals with a Veil Generator, build Survival Barracks and repel successive Thrall and Cinderling attacks. **Optional:** share scarce supplies with two neighboring settlements instead of commandeering their generators. **Failure:** the hospital and command position both destroyed. **Story:** Voss protects millions by decree; Awan insists that emergency powers require an ending date.

#### H03. The Glass Fields
**Theatre:** early Veilglass mining belt. **Primary:** build a Fracture Refinery, watch an Industrial Harvester complete two mine-return-unload cycles, and field Bulwark tanks from a Motor Pool. **Optional:** protect miners working beside enriched seams instead of maximizing immediate yield. **Failure:** no operational refining chain survives the assault. **Story:** Veilglass gives humanity a chance to fight and a new temptation to exploit its own people.

#### H04. Teeth in the Crystal
**Theatre:** Glass Fields eastern dig. **Primary:** rescue a mining crew, deploy Rocketeers and destroy Demon Devourers raiding the ore road. **Optional:** recover the dead crew's sensor data before the next tremor. **Failure:** all rescue carriers lost. **Story:** the recovered signals contain an impossible ancient human script embedded inside the crystal.

#### H05. The Last Dominion
**Theatre:** contested refuge corridors. **Primary:** establish an MCV forward base, defend two evacuation exits and join a Bastion artillery company to Union scout detachments. **Optional:** recover and repair a damaged Bulwark rather than abandon it. **Failure:** the command yard falls before both corridors are secured. **Story:** three incompatible survivor coalitions sign a deliberately temporary military charter.

### ACT II: THE WAR OF CLAIMS (H06–H10)

#### H06. Smoke over Meridian
**Theatre:** Salt Meridian reservoir. **Primary:** secure pumping stations against Cinderlings and keep an Industrial Harvester alive long enough to build a second refinery. **Optional:** stop a retreating refinery demolition team to preserve fresh water. **Failure:** all pumps poisoned or command lost. **Story:** someone is selling human settlement coordinates to the Court of Whispers.

#### H07. Do Not Look Up
**Theatre:** Broken Choir sanctuary district. **Primary:** disable the Seraphic Synod's All-Seeing Halo, penetrate a Celestial Archive and withdraw with the order records. **Optional:** spare the hospice below an Aegis siege corridor. **Failure:** archive team eliminated or evidence carrier destroyed. **Story:** the Synod's occupation directive has an impossible authorisation mark. **Crossover:** Angel A08 sees the same engagement.

#### H08. Broken Choir Rising
**Theatre:** ruined rail terminus. **Primary:** build a defensive Motor Pool beside a temporary railhead, survive two celestial probes and destroy an infernal raider forge. **Optional:** open a neutral passage for displaced Angels who reject coercive orders. **Failure:** railhead lost. **Story:** an Unshackled messenger offers testimony proving that parts of Heaven dispute the edict.

#### H09. The Listening Dead
**Theatre:** flooded broadcast spires. **Primary:** recover recordings from two relay stations while repelling Court Hexers and fielding Wasteland Rangers. **Optional:** broadcast unedited casualty records instead of only military intelligence. **Failure:** both recording carriers captured. **Story:** Sister Calen and Observer Niko, the Two Witnesses, begin exposing abuses by all three realms.

#### H10. The Endless Descent
**Theatre:** Ashen Causeway. **Primary:** defend three timed refugee convoy departures with Bulwarks and Siege Cannons, then destroy the Flesh Forge supporting the infernal advance. **Optional:** extract an isolated Ash Templar company. **Failure:** convoy loss threshold reached. **Story:** an Iron Legion officer quietly refuses to execute fleeing civilians. **Crossover:** Demon D12 commands part of the opposing assault.

### ACT III: THE FALSE EDICTS (H11–H15)

#### H11. The King's Road
**Theatre:** Crown Vale outer motorway. **Primary:** escort a mobile refinery across a highway and deny three enemy ambush routes by constructing forward defenses. **Optional:** clear a road for civilians instead of an easier tank supply bypass. **Failure:** convoy core or construction support lost. **Story:** Voss proposes permanent wartime control of the roads after the immediate crisis.

#### H12. A War of Borrowed Gods
**Theatre:** Black Sun research compound. **Primary:** seize a compromised human experimental yard before the Court steals its anti-entity ammunition and destroy enemy Hexer cells. **Optional:** extract noncombatant technicians with the data. **Failure:** the research archive and evac shuttle destroyed. **Story:** the Ashen Covenant admits that forbidden knowledge can be both a tool of liberation and an instrument of torture.

#### H13. The Pale Road
**Theatre:** plague-stricken refugee belt. **Primary:** restore power to three quarantine hospitals while holding against demonic swarms and hostile opportunist human militia. **Optional:** refuse an unlawful order to use an inhabited settlement as a firebreak. **Failure:** medical network collapses. **Story:** rumours of the Pale Rider spread; the event is an apocalyptic environmental crisis, not a controllable cavalry unit.

#### H14. The False Sanctuary
**Theatre:** Salt Meridian enclave. **Primary:** secure the waterworks and expose the Court's identity-seal contracts. **Optional choice:** negotiate civilian self-government, or seize the enriched Veilglass field under emergency military law. **Failure:** waterworks destroyed or all Witness Kin evacuated into enemy custody. **Story:** **Trust** or **Control** is recorded for H25; each has strategic consequences, but neither is a generic resource bonus.

#### H15. The Dominion Fractures
**Theatre:** emergency government capital. **Primary:** hold the public broadcast hall against a rogue Directorate battalion and Demon infiltration without destroying the city's power grid. **Optional:** capture its commander alive and publish the disputed orders. **Failure:** the broadcast hall falls. **Story:** Voss, Awan and Vey quarrel over whose consent counts in an emergency; the player's subfaction changes negotiations and available reinforcements.

### ACT IV: THE WITNESS LEDGER (H16–H20)

#### H16. The Two Witnesses
**Theatre:** mountain signal observatory. **Primary:** defend Sister Calen and Observer Niko long enough to transmit uncensored testimony while moving Veilglass along two supply routes. **Optional:** keep the second transmitter intact, permitting later contact with Unshackled units. **Failure:** both transmitters lost before testimony is sent. **Story:** records from the two realms share exactly the same forged passage.

#### H17. The Unwritten Names
**Theatre:** subterranean Witness Kin settlement. **Primary:** evacuate a community targeted by all three intelligence services, build defenses and extract its memory-keepers. **Optional:** allow them to choose their own destination, forfeiting a convenient garrison. **Failure:** all memory-keepers captured or killed. **Story:** the ancient covenant is a community-held rule, not the property of a chosen child or bloodline.

#### H18. The Lazarus Test
**Theatre:** desert launch complex. **Primary:** assemble the first orbital/ground relay network with mined Veilglass, defend prototype equipment and disable a Court sabotage unit. **Optional:** perform a slower nonlethal calibration before firing. **Failure:** all relays destroyed. **Story:** Lazarus can cut foreign conduits, but firing at full power would strand civilians at the seams. **Feature status:** full Lazarus superweapon mechanics planned.

#### H19. Broken Covenant
**Theatre:** old archive beneath Broken Choir. **Primary:** stop an Ashen Covenant splinter faction from coercively testing Witness Kin, seize the missing ledger clause and repel Court raiders. **Optional:** preserve consented scientific observations and evacuate test subjects. **Failure:** ledger carrier and all witnesses lost. **Story:** Vhal fabricated both war orders; lawful guardianship requires the living's freely given consent.

#### H20. A Voice Without a Throne
**Theatre:** Broken Choir communications district. **Primary:** transmit the restored clause to multiple human and celestial receivers, defending separate antenna sites against Synod diehards and the Court. **Optional:** offer Orathiel's guardians access to the broadcast. **Failure:** all relays destroyed. **Story:** victory cannot come simply by winning the information war; the human command must decide how it will use the truth.

### ACT V: THE HARROW OF DAWN (H21–H25)

#### H21. Siege of Crown Vale
**Theatre:** original evacuation city. **Primary:** retake a divided industrial district and protect its refinery system against an Infernal iron column. **Optional:** rescue Voss's political opponents from a prison without losing the forward base. **Failure:** city command lost. **Story:** the coalition's founding city becomes proof of what military victory has cost the living.

#### H22. The Reluctant Alliance
**Theatre:** approach to Empty Gate. **Primary:** escort a shared convoy of humans and Unshackled defenders through Court ambushes and build a joint holding line. **Optional:** disarm an overzealous Angel strike force rather than destroy it. **Failure:** the treaty convoy annihilated. **Story:** the first real agreement between Heaven and humanity is negotiated, not imposed; prior Trust/Control choices alter dialogue.

#### H23. Lazarus Ascendant
**Theatre:** Harrow of Dawn perimeter. **Primary:** erect a protected Veilglass collection chain, hold three conventional relay stations and defeat two infernal siege groups. **Optional:** preserve a civilian evacuation corridor instead of converting it to artillery access. **Failure:** all relays lost. **Story:** the player receives the order to fire but learns there are two target solutions: separation or monopoly.

#### H24. The Last Consent
**Theatre:** Harrow's living settlements. **Primary:** break the Court's false voting seals, protect Tamar and the Witness delegates, and move civilians through safe exits. **Optional:** accept a temporary halt in artillery fire while neutral witnesses inspect the evacuation. **Failure:** witness assembly destroyed. **Story:** a legal victory without the consent of the governed would reproduce Vhal's crime.

#### H25. We Remain
**Theatre:** Harrow of Dawn central Seamfall. **Primary:** maintain physical Veilglass harvesting, defend command infrastructure, stop the Edict and Ninth Mouth countdowns, and defeat the final contested force. **Final choice:** **Open Earth**, a limited Lazarus severance with voluntary reconstruction; or **Iron Earth**, a human monopoly maintained by authoritarian orbital power. **Failure:** relay network destroyed or rival completion timer expires. **Story:** humanity determines whether its independence extends to the civilians it claims to protect.

## CAMPAIGN II: WAR IN HEAVEN (ANGELS)

**Player role:** a celestial field commander aligned with Seraphic Synod, Order of the Gate or The Unshackled. **Main voices:** High Legate Seraviel, Custodian Orathiel, Captain Maeron and Tamar Rell. **Moral arc:** duty → doubt → obedience versus guardianship by consent.

### ACT I: THE BREAKING (A01–A05)

#### A01. The Call to Arms
**Theatre:** Empty Gate approach. **Primary:** deploy the Celestial MCV, power a Radiant Spire and hold the threshold against first infernal raiders. **Optional:** shelter mortals caught beneath the descent. **Failure:** Gate anchor destroyed. **Story:** Heaven's orders stop updating during the Seventh Silence; the archive issues a suspicious emergency fallback.

#### A02. The Choir Unbroken
**Theatre:** refugee quarter at the Gate. **Primary:** defend human sanctuary routes with a Choir Citadel and Watchers while evacuations proceed. **Optional:** let the civilians choose their own destination, despite complicating the defensive perimeter. **Failure:** main sanctuary falls. **Story:** Orathiel and Seraviel disagree about the meaning of protection.

#### A03. A Glass of Wrath
**Theatre:** Glass Fields. **Primary:** establish a Sanctification Crucible, perform two complete Gatherer unloading cycles and build an Aegis Engine to resist Blood Brood assaults. **Optional:** purify a dangerous enriched seam at the cost of time. **Failure:** economy destroyed beyond recovery. **Story:** the Host's archives have erased references to the Witness Ledger.

#### A04. Wings over Dust
**Theatre:** Crown Vale outskirts. **Primary:** protect three descending reinforcement zones while clearing Infernal Skitters and avoiding civilian shelters. **Optional:** escort human doctors, who can independently vouch for the Host's mercy. **Failure:** all landing zones disabled. **Story:** human observers do not distinguish protecting angels from the Synod's occupying armies.

#### A05. The Watchers' Debt
**Theatre:** ancient First Watch ruin. **Primary:** seize a subterranean archive before Court Hexers corrupt it, constructing a forward Heavenforge to breach defenses. **Optional:** preserve a sealed mural showing mortals refusing a celestial command. **Failure:** all archive chambers ruined. **Story:** some fallen beings were punished for giving mortals forbidden weapons; others were punished for defying cruel instructions.

### ACT II: THE WAR OF CLAIMS (A06–A10)

#### A06. The Emperor of Ash
**Theatre:** Sable Crown. **Primary:** disable Iron Legion anti-air, field Judicators and Aegis Engines, then destroy a forward Hellreaver siege forge. **Optional:** spare prisoners who disavow Vhal's orders. **Failure:** siege gate fully deployed. **Story:** infernal prisoners show their own forged claim to Earth. **Crossover:** D05 describes the surviving core foundry, not the destroyed forward outpost.

#### A07. Writ of Fire
**Theatre:** Celestial command bastion. **Primary:** defend three command-signature archives while purging a Court infiltration unit. **Optional:** copy, rather than burn, all disputed warrants. **Failure:** authentication core lost. **Story:** Seraviel's purported command carries an inactive signature, but she argues delay would doom millions.

#### A08. The Mortal Refusal
**Theatre:** Broken Choir sanctuary district. **Primary:** secure All-Seeing Halo sites and contain Human Bulwark and Rocketeer resistance, keeping a functional supply chain. **Optional:** spare the hospice and offer a real evacuation corridor instead of forcible relocation. **Failure:** all command and radar positions lost. **Story:** the surviving human evidence contradicts the Synod edict. **Crossover:** H07 sees the battle as an occupation.

#### A09. The Weight of Wings
**Theatre:** Crown Vale refugee air corridor. **Primary:** protect a convoy of human medical transports from Demon raiders and a rogue Synod demolition group. **Optional:** allow volunteers to return to defend their homes rather than requisition them as guides. **Failure:** too few transports escape. **Story:** Maeron openly accuses the celestial hierarchy of substituting compliance for mercy.

#### A10. Orathiel's Oath
**Theatre:** Ashen Causeway. **Primary:** hold a line against infernal Hellreavers, escort human refugee vehicles and protect a Gatekeeper strongpoint. **Optional:** bargain with a human commander for joint fire corridors. **Failure:** the escape route cut before the convoy leaves. **Story:** a celestial authority can honour a promise made to a mortal even when superiors object. **Crossover:** overlaps H10.

### ACT III: THE FALSE EDICTS (A11–A15)

#### A11. The Fallen Archive
**Theatre:** hidden Unshackled library. **Primary:** locate copies of the old command hierarchy, defeat Hexers and defend scholars before a Synod purge arrives. **Optional:** recover a testimony about the First Watch that would embarrass the Host. **Failure:** all original tablets destroyed. **Story:** the First Covenant limited outside rule by design.

#### A12. The Unshackling
**Theatre:** vaults under Broken Choir. **Primary:** survive the Synod purge and Court raid, protect Maeron's Penitents and evacuate documentary evidence. **Choice:** seize the records as heresy or recognise Maeron's witness and disobey the suspect edict. **Failure:** every document carrier eliminated. **Story:** celestial unanimity was manufactured by deleting dissent from the record.

#### A13. Ten Thousand Eyes
**Theatre:** Angel observatory network. **Primary:** rebuild an All-Seeing Halo intelligence network without exposing civilian positions to the Court. **Optional:** stop tracking neutral towns and accept reduced scouting vision. **Failure:** all observatories captured. **Story:** observation can protect refugees or enable their imprisonment.

#### A14. The Trumpets of Dust
**Theatre:** Salt Meridian. **Primary:** protect evacuation and drinking-water infrastructure during staged, clearly telegraphed apocalyptic storm waves, then eliminate the Court's contamination source. **Optional:** divert power to human shelters rather than static wards. **Failure:** water source lost. **Story:** trumpet imagery becomes battlefield catastrophe, not a claim that Heaven caused every disaster.

#### A15. The Silent Choir
**Theatre:** remote celestial basilica. **Primary:** defend an independent choir while its archivists inspect the alleged edict, then defeat zealot attackers. **Optional:** broadcast the conflicting verdict without censorship. **Failure:** choir archive destroyed. **Story:** the silence from the Throne remains unexplained; angels cannot use uncertainty as proof of permission.

### ACT IV: THE WITNESS LEDGER (A16–A20)

#### A16. The Empty Gate
**Theatre:** threshold complex. **Primary:** hold shield pylons, escort refugees and harvest Veilglass needed to keep defenses powered against the Court's breach engines. **Choice:** close immediately with civilians outside, or extend the defense so they can escape. **Failure:** all Gate anchors lost. **Story:** the genuine surviving directive is to hear living testimony.

#### A17. The Names Not Burned
**Theatre:** Witness Kin refuge. **Primary:** guard a human delegation as its elders speak before an Angel tribunal, withstanding both infernal and fanatic interruption. **Optional:** record dissenting testimonies rather than a unanimous decree. **Failure:** witness assembly silenced. **Story:** the covenant is held by communities, not by a celestial succession of rulers.

#### A18. The Seraphic Tribunal
**Theatre:** Synod citadel. **Primary:** protect a hearing and defend the evidence vault while rival wings of the Host contest its legality. **Optional:** persuade a Seraphim commander to stand down without annihilating her troops. **Failure:** records destroyed before hearing concludes. **Story:** Seraviel learns that her command was falsified but fears surrendering authority will unleash chaos.

#### A19. A Choir Divided
**Theatre:** broken High Choir skybridge. **Primary:** stop a Synod siege of Gate defenders, hold a Celestial Archive and prevent Court sabotage. **Optional:** rescue the purging force's wounded rather than pursuing its retreat. **Failure:** Gate command eliminated. **Story:** different heavenly orders can disagree passionately without either becoming demons.

#### A20. The Living Testament
**Theatre:** Broken Choir relay complex. **Primary:** transmit the authenticated covenant to all three major Hosts, protecting Veilglass-powered broadcast relays. **Optional:** send a copy to human councils before warning the Synod. **Failure:** every relay destroyed. **Story:** an order has authority only if it can withstand truthful scrutiny.

### ACT V: THE HARROW OF DAWN (A21–A25)

#### A21. The Ninefold March
**Theatre:** Harrow approach. **Primary:** reunite three isolated celestial columns, defend their Gatherers and crush a Hellreaver roadblock. **Optional:** permit Unshackled scouts to lead rather than demanding public repentance. **Failure:** all field command anchors lost. **Story:** ancient choirs reunite temporarily while debating the purpose of their march.

#### A22. Refuge of the Unshackled
**Theatre:** Empty Gate rear sanctuary. **Primary:** repel a joint Court and rogue Synod attack while evacuating mortal and Unshackled refugees. **Optional:** let human engineers service celestial wards under equal command terms. **Failure:** sanctuary lost. **Story:** angels who break a false order can still be loyal to the good it claimed to serve.

#### A23. The Fire That Judges
**Theatre:** Harrow outer bastions. **Primary:** construct a defended Celestial supply network and defeat incoming human artillery and Demon swarms without destroying the settlements they defend. **Optional:** disable a Lazarus relay rather than raze its hospital-adjacent power plant. **Failure:** all bastions fall. **Story:** the Edict of Dawn may purify the seams or imprison the planet.

#### A24. Before the Edict
**Theatre:** Harrow witness amphitheatre. **Primary:** secure tribunal approaches, keep Tamar alive and prevent Vhal from substituting counterfeit witnesses. **Optional:** grant adversarial speakers from human settlements and infernal defectors a hearing. **Failure:** testimony corrupted or site destroyed. **Story:** the commander must decide whether listening is a duty or a weakness.

#### A25. Judgment Deferred
**Theatre:** Harrow of Dawn central Seamfall. **Primary:** survive three-front attacks, retain Gatherer income, stop the Ninth Mouth and take the Edict command nodes. **Final choice:** **The Open Gate**, a voluntary guardian pact that preserves human rule, or **Radiant Quarantine**, a closed, safe but authoritarian world beneath the Host. **Failure:** the Gate collapses or Vhal completes his claim. **Story:** a celestial victory reveals whether mercy and freedom can coexist.

## CAMPAIGN III: THE NINTH MOUTH (DEMONS)

**Player role:** an infernal general serving Iron Legions, Blood Brood or Court of Whispers. **Main voices:** Archduke Azrakh, Broodmother Tzura, Chancellor Vhal and Lady Nerezza. **Moral arc:** conquest → recognition of infernal slavery → a throne or freedom from thrones.

### ACT I: THE BREAKING (D01–D05)

#### D01. Hunger Wakes
**Theatre:** Sable Crown foothills. **Primary:** deploy a Demonic construction nucleus, raise a Furnace Heart and Birthing Pit, and destroy the human border watch. **Optional:** free captive Thralls from a collapsed caravan. **Failure:** spawning ground destroyed. **Story:** commanders promise freedom after victory, and their veterans know the promise has been made before.

#### D02. Seeds of Ruin
**Theatre:** Glass Fields. **Primary:** establish a Maw of Want, complete two full Devourer mine-return-unload cycles and defeat a defended human refinery. **Optional:** avoid corrupting the only potable water route to secure local neutral guides. **Failure:** economy and remaining production nests destroyed. **Story:** Hell can consume the world, but cannot conquer ashes that sustain nothing.

#### D03. The Gate Eaters
**Theatre:** celestial supply road. **Primary:** ambush Angel Gatherers, disable ward pylons and capture navigation codes. **Optional:** rescue infernal labourers sent into the line of fire as decoys. **Failure:** all raiding detachments destroyed or convoy escapes. **Story:** the stolen codes contain an ancient human phrase that Hell's scholars cannot translate.

#### D04. Shackle and Spur
**Theatre:** infernal labour encampment. **Primary:** reinforce a threatened Flesh Forge and break a rebellion by a rival contract-lord. **Optional:** unbind the defeated labourers rather than absorbing them as enslaved reinforcements. **Failure:** forge lost. **Story:** bondage is an economic institution within Hell, defended by ambitious devils and hated by much of its rank and file.

#### D05. Iron and Horn
**Theatre:** Sable Crown core foundry. **Primary:** protect production as Ravagers and Hellreavers are forged, defeat a forward Angel siege and secure the route to the Crown Below. **Optional:** release beasts and miners held as collateral. **Failure:** all primary forges destroyed. **Story:** Azrakh rejects Vhal's legal maneuvers but trusts too much in the sanctity of infernal conquest. **Crossover:** A06 destroys an external siege annex, not this central foundry.

### ACT II: THE WAR OF CLAIMS (D06–D10)

#### D06. A Crown of Cinders
**Theatre:** Black Furnace Steps. **Primary:** claim a rival duke's Veilglass nodes through a conventional MCV, refinery and Hellreaver offensive. **Optional:** secure surrender from lesser captains, reducing reprisals. **Failure:** assault headquarters destroyed. **Story:** the infernal nobility funds an endless war by mortgaging the freedom of unborn servants.

#### D07. The Crown Below
**Theatre:** infernal border province. **Primary:** seize or destroy a forge-lord's citadel and prevent Court Hexers from replacing the army's command seals. **Optional:** negotiate the surrender of bound soldiers. **Failure:** command sigil captured. **Story:** the supposed ancient deed granting Hell ownership of Earth is a crude forgery.

#### D08. The Howling Flood
**Theatre:** Salt Meridian approaches. **Primary:** escort a Maw of Want and Devourers across a river delta, defeat a human Rocketeer screen and secure enriched deposits. **Optional:** keep the delta unpoisoned, preserving future food and crossing routes. **Failure:** supply caravan lost. **Story:** Azrakh calls mercy an expensive luxury; Tzura calls fertile land military infrastructure.

#### D09. Children of the Furnace
**Theatre:** Blood Brood hive network. **Primary:** rescue three broods from a Court liquidation order while defending Birthing Pits against hostile demons. **Optional:** free a broodmother's captured offspring before the assault. **Failure:** all brood nests razed. **Story:** Tzura's offspring are soldiers by birth but insist that they should not be treated as replaceable ammunition.

#### D10. The Whispers of Debt
**Theatre:** Salt Meridian mercantile enclave. **Primary:** protect Court contract presses, intercept a human investigation and consolidate Veilglass trade routes. **Optional:** falsify proof to mislead a rival lord or preserve the original contracts for Nerezza. **Failure:** all presses seized. **Story:** Nerezza learns Vhal's laws are written to bind whole populations who never signed them. **Crossover:** sets up H14.

### ACT III: THE FALSE EDICTS (D11–D15)

#### D11. The Broken Treaty
**Theatre:** Broken Choir perimeter. **Primary:** guard a neutral summit between infernal defectors and human survivors while repelling Court assassins. **Optional:** deliver an honest copy of a disputed land claim to the human envoys. **Failure:** all delegates killed. **Story:** an infernal oath can mean something when both parties are free to refuse it.

#### D12. Flesh Against Steel
**Theatre:** Ashen Causeway. **Primary:** assault Bastion defenses with Hellreavers and Ravagers, destroy a forward Motor Pool and hold a viable retreat route. **Optional:** refuse Vhal's order to kill civilian convoys, securing aid from an independent infernal officer. **Failure:** every siege force destroyed. **Story:** the player sees how propaganda describes fleeing families as combatants. **Crossover:** H10 defends these convoys, A10 also intervenes.

#### D13. The Poisoned Meridian
**Theatre:** river junction. **Primary:** repel a Blood Brood infestation that threatens the Legion's own drinking route and eliminate a hostile toxin source. **Optional:** preserve the waterworks rather than turning them into a permanent corruption engine. **Failure:** all wells poisoned. **Story:** demons discover the political usefulness and self-destructive cost of scorched-earth tactics.

#### D14. Court of Masks
**Theatre:** Broken Choir archive-city. **Primary:** infiltrate rival archives, manipulate human and angel scouts into attacking a false convoy, and extract a Witness Kin scroll. **Optional:** preserve both the original and forged documents for later comparison. **Failure:** courier network exposed and destroyed. **Story:** the Court's counterfeit edicts are the real engine of the three-sided war.

#### D15. Blasphemous Engine
**Theatre:** Lazarus desert launch complex. **Primary:** shut down human targeting relays, break Bastion artillery lines and seize the prototype's Veilglass capacitor. **Optional:** evacuate bound infernal labourers and human engineers before sabotaging the site. **Failure:** Lazarus prototype launches successfully against the invasion route. **Story:** humans can shut the cosmic gateways, and Vhal is concealing the limits of his supposed liberation.

### ACT IV: THE WITNESS LEDGER (D16–D20)

#### D16. Ungiven Names
**Theatre:** Witness Kin burial labyrinth. **Primary:** protect an infernal scholar who can read ancient settlement registers from three Court strike teams. **Optional:** let surviving keepers retain custody of their records. **Failure:** scholar and registries destroyed. **Story:** the old law bars perpetual claims made on behalf of people who did not consent.

#### D17. The Debt of Heaven
**Theatre:** abandoned First Watch station. **Primary:** defeat a celestial garrison that holds the other half of the contract record and escape before Synod reinforcements arrive. **Optional:** save an Unshackled prisoner who can confirm its provenance. **Failure:** contract record lost. **Story:** both celestial and infernal histories altered the same ancient event for political convenience.

#### D18. The Court's Price
**Theatre:** Court of Whispers archive. **Primary:** survive Vhal's ambush, destroy three counterfeit contract presses and extract the original Witness Ledger fragments. **Choice:** send the record to Azrakh for an empire or to Nerezza for a genuine revolt. **Failure:** archive seized by Vhal and the player's command destroyed. **Story:** the Ninth Mouth will bind every traveller directly to Vhal.

#### D19. Crownless
**Theatre:** infernal political capital. **Primary:** defeat the Court's loyal bodyguard and release captured commanders while keeping the army supplied. **Optional:** avoid razing the labour district, where families of enslaved Thralls live. **Failure:** rebel command compound destroyed. **Story:** without Vhal's promised throne, the Legion must discover what its army is actually for.

#### D20. The Oldest Gate
**Theatre:** first infernal crossing beneath Empty Gate. **Primary:** stabilize one retreat route with Devourer income, destroy Vhal's false anchor and fight back hostile Synod sentinels. **Optional:** free the ancient sentries on both sides from automatic binding orders. **Failure:** old gate collapses before the army exits. **Story:** the Witness Ledger also protects demons from fraudulent infernal command chains, without erasing their responsibility for past crimes.

### ACT V: THE HARROW OF DAWN (D21–D25)

#### D21. The Harrow March
**Theatre:** volcanic approach to the seventh Seamfall. **Primary:** establish a forward Maw of Want, build two production lines, and force three defended passes under Human Siege Cannon fire. **Optional:** negotiate passage for refugees who wish to leave the war. **Failure:** the marching command nucleus destroyed. **Story:** a warlord's conquest becomes possible precisely when its army considers rebellion.

#### D22. Ash and Kin
**Theatre:** Blood Brood sanctuary. **Primary:** save endangered hatcheries from Vhal's punitive strike while simultaneously defending a Devourer corridor. **Optional:** help human civilians sharing the nearest spring, despite objections from Legion officers. **Failure:** all brood colonies razed. **Story:** Tzura argues that the right not to be enslaved must apply even to creatures bred to serve.

#### D23. The False Monarch
**Theatre:** Vhal's mobile throne-fortress. **Primary:** destroy the forged-claim relays, defeat elite Hexer garrisons and recover authentic command signatures. **Optional:** spare the Court's conscripted scribes. **Failure:** command signatures permanently recaptured. **Story:** Vhal announces that liberation and conquest are identical because he will personally define freedom.

#### D24. The Last Chain
**Theatre:** Harrow's underground covenant machinery. **Primary:** use Veilglass power to break or transfer three binding anchors while holding off celestial and infernal interference. **Optional:** free imprisoned Witness Kin and enemy deserters. **Failure:** all anchor sites lost. **Story:** the player may inherit Hell's power intact or destroy the system that produces automatic obedience.

#### D25. The Shape of Freedom
**Theatre:** Harrow of Dawn central Seamfall. **Primary:** maintain Infernal Devourer supply, defeat Vhal's guard and repel both Lazarus and the Edict while preparing the Ninth Mouth. **Final choice:** **Crown of Ash**, rule Earth and Hell through captured bondage; or **Unbound Fire**, invert the gateway and release infernal servants from their inherited chains. **Failure:** Ninth Mouth lost or enemy final strike resolves. **Story:** the final irony is whether the victorious rebel chooses to become a master.

## SUBFACTION ADAPTATION WITHOUT 225 DIFFERENT MAPS

Each of the nine subfactions plays its parent campaign's **same 25 numbered missions** with relevant briefing variants, one subfaction-exclusive unit where unlocked, optional mission objectives and small changes in allies and dialogue. **Total campaign missions remain 75, not 225.**

- **Seraphic Synod**: Seraviel's legal certainty; Seraphim; alternate loyalty/mercy objectives and an ending centered on responsibility for coercion.
- **Order of the Gate**: Orathiel's protection oath; Gatekeeper; escort and shield-oriented side objectives.
- **The Unshackled**: Maeron's dissent; Penitent; infiltration routes and proof-preservation rewards.
