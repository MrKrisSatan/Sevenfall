# SEVENFALL: THE WAR FOR CREATION
## Opening Cinematic: THE SEVENTH SILENCE

**Status:** Narrative / cinematic script. Not implemented or playable in OpenRA.  
**Placement:** Plays once before the first campaign selection. Optional replay in the menu.  
**Target duration:** approximately 3–4 minutes with deliberate silent beats.  
**Principal narrator:** Tamar Rell, witness-librarian and keeper of fragments of the ancient Witness Ledger.  
**Music:** original, restrained low strings and distant voices building into a full choir, brass, percussion and industrial rhythm. All recorded dialogue, sound and imagery must be original or licensed.

### 00:00 | The Sixth Warning

**BLACK SCREEN.**

A faint radio signal fights through static. A siren starts, fails, and starts again.

**CROWN VALE CIVIL DEFENCE (RADIO, DISTORTED)**  
"All roads out of the city are closed. Repeat, all roads are closed. Those still inside the evacuation zone must find shelter. May God help us."

A heartbeat. Then the first image: an ordinary kitchen at dawn. A cup falls from someone's hand, but never reaches the floor before the picture cuts.

**TAMAR RELL (V.O., QUIET)**  
"When the end came, no one could agree what to call it. The faithful said judgment. The frightened said invasion. The governments called it a crisis, until there were no governments left to correct them."

**MONTAGE:** Flags raised above abandoned capitals. Armoured columns firing into smoke. Shelves stripped bare. Fields blackened under a yellow sky. Unmarked graves beside motorways. Crowds looking up as the sun darkens.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"The warnings had come one after another. Conquest. War. Hunger. Death. The cries of those no one had protected. Then the heavens themselves began to break."

Above Crown Vale, clouds twist around a black sun. A crack of gold spreads from horizon to horizon.

**TAMAR (V.O., ALMOST A WHISPER)**  
"And then the seventh seal was opened."

### 00:46 | The Silence

**ALL SOUND CUTS.**

Traffic lights freeze mid-change. A hospital monitor holds a single note with no sound. An aircraft hangs motionless against the sun.

Far beyond Earth, ranks of armoured angels stand before a cathedral suspended over a sea of impossible light. Seven enormous rings cease turning. A choir opens its mouths, but no song comes.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"For half an hour, Heaven was silent."

High Legate **SERAVIEL** turns toward the luminous archive behind her.

**SERAVIEL**  
"Has the command been confirmed?"

**VOICE OF THE ARCHIVE**  
"No new command has been received."

A scroll of living light illuminates across Seraviel's armour. Its final lines are obscured from the audience.

**SERAVIEL**  
"Then the last order stands. Ready the Host."

A vast gate opens. Thousands of spears lower in perfect unison.

### 01:20 | Three Realms Collide

**SOUND RETURNS AS A THUNDERCLAP.**

Seven colossal scars burn across the world. From one descends a procession of gilded war engines and winged soldiers. Some lift fallen civilians from burning streets. Others raise walls of radiant light around whole cities, sealing their inhabitants inside.

A CELESTIAL HERALD broadcasts over the skyline.

**CELESTIAL HERALD**  
"Lay down your weapons. Enter the sanctuaries. All life within the protected boundaries shall be preserved."

A woman holds her child at a checkpoint.

**WOMAN**  
"And if we don't want to go?"

The Herald's halo turns. No answer.

Deep underground, black iron gates split open. Horns roar. **ARCHDUKE AZRAKH** leads the Iron Legions upward as Ravager Beasts and Hellreavers climb through the breach. Behind them, the Blood Brood spills through the cracks like a living tide.

**AZRAKH**  
"The road above is open. Let the kingdoms of Heaven dispute their orders. We shall take what we can hold."

In the darkness behind the army, **CHANCELLOR VHAL** smiles without showing his face.

**VHAL (SOFTLY)**  
"And every kingdom must pay for its passage."

An infernal Thrall breaks a fellow soldier's shackle. An overseer drags them apart. The army marches on.

### 02:02 | The Glass Fields

Blue-violet crystals burst from fractured roads and hillsides. Gold and blood-red light pulse through them. A Celestial Gatherer approaches a vein under Watcher escort. An Infernal Devourer tears crystal from the ground and returns to a breathing Maw of Want. Human engineers gather glittering shards beside a ruined refinery.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"The boundary between worlds had been torn apart. Where it bled into the earth, something new took shape. A stone filled with the power to build, to heal, to imprison... or to destroy."

A human scientist places a shard in a rough testing chamber. A dormant generator comes alive.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"We called it **Veilglass**."

**CUT BETWEEN THREE WAR MACHINES:** a Sanctification Crucible lifting pale crystal into celestial light; a Maw of Want swallowing the same mineral in infernal flame; a Fracture Refinery pouring the first charge into a battered human power grid.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"Heaven would use it to seal the wounds. Hell would use it to tear them wider. We needed it to keep the lights on."

A human field gun, powered by Veilglass, fires into an attacking demonic siege beast. For the first time, the creature falls.

### 02:38 | The Refusal

**SHELTER DELTA.** A tactical map glows on the side of a makeshift command vehicle. Red and gold enemy markers close around the last human evacuation routes.

**IDRIS AWAN**  
"Two generators left. One working Motor Pool. The crystal fields are outside the perimeter."

**MARSHAL MARA VOSS**  
"If we move the Harvesters, they'll attack."

**IDRIS**  
"They're attacking already."

Voss looks toward the exhausted people lining up for food beside a field hospital.

**VOSS**  
"Then build the refinery. Arm the volunteers. And leave a road open for the families."

**MONTAGE BUILDS WITH MUSIC:** a Human MCV unfolds into a Construction Yard. Veil Generators hum awake. Riflemen take positions behind sandbags. Rocketeers sight down a ruined avenue. Bulwark Tanks grind forward. Across the battlefield, the Choir Citadels of Heaven and Birthing Pits of Hell rise to answer them.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"The angels said they had come to save us. The devils said the world was theirs by ancient right. They brought laws, promises and armies."

At the edge of Broken Choir, Tamar examines a shattered inscription beneath a fallen statue. Its central words have been deliberately scraped away.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"But in the ruins, beneath all their decrees, there was a question older than the war."

A little girl looks up at the armed silhouettes filling the sky.

**CHILD**  
"Who told them they could take our home?"

Tamar closes her hand around a piece of inscribed stone.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"No one had thought to ask us."

### 03:26 | The War for Creation

**THREE RAPID IMAGES:**

**SERAVIEL**, before an army of golden wings:  
"Creation will be preserved."

**AZRAKH**, as infernal siege engines rise through smoke:  
"Earth will kneel."

**VOSS**, standing among ordinary soldiers as her artillery begins to fire:  
"This is our world. Hold the line."

A radiant lance falls from the sky. An infernal breach tears open beneath it. Between them, human artillery flashes. The three explosions meet in absolute white.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

**TAMAR (V.O., STEADY)**  
"They would remember it as the Apocalypse."

A distant voice, neither celestial nor infernal, exhales through the silence.

**TAMAR (V.O.)**  
"We would remember what came after."

## Implementation and continuity notes

1. This prologue is **shared**. All three choices should feel playable and morally substantial, not a disguised hero/villain selector.
2. The apparent emergency Celestial order and Vhal's confidence **foreshadow** later revelations. Do not expose the forged orders or the Witness Ledger's consent clause outright in the cinematic.
3. The three crystal processors and harvesting vehicles match the existing Sevenfall faction design and present the *physical* Veilglass resource economy; these shots are scripted cinematics, not claims that these animations are implemented.
4. Keep Seraviel's motivation as protection through control, Azrakh's as conquest and martial order, and Voss's as survival with a threatened commitment to civilian sovereignty.
5. Tamar speaks as a **witness**, not an omniscient narrator. The final historical outcome is unknown, since H25/A25/D25 are alternative campaign endings.
6. No footage, voice recordings, musical cues or dialogue from *The Prophecy*, *Darksiders*, *Diablo* or other copyrighted media may be reused. Biblical imagery is adapted as original dark fantasy.
