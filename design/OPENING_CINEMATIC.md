# The Seventh Silence — opening cinematic

**Current narrative:** six campaigns,150 planned missions, nine peoples. This is a screenplay/design specification, not a rendered movie or playable campaign. [The story bible](STORY_BIBLE.md) and [mission atlas](CAMPAIGN_MISSIONS.md) define the current continuity. The earlier three-campaign screenplay is preserved in [the archive](archive/OPENING_CINEMATIC_THREE_CAMPAIGN.md).

## Shared prologue: The War for Creation

**0:00–0:20 — A world holding its breath.** Black screen. A low mechanical pulse becomes a human heartbeat, then stops. Over the dark, TAMAR RELL speaks: “We waited for the last command. What came was silence.” The SEVENTH SILENCE spreads across a crowded city; lights fail, prayers continue, radio operators call for answers.

**0:20–0:45 — Three claims.** The sky fractures above the Ashen Causeway. SERAVIEL watches the CELESTIAL HOST descend beneath an unconfirmed edict. Far below, AZRAKH receives a contract bearing the INFERNAL LEGIONS' royal seals. VHAL closes a second ledger before either messenger can see it. Neither scene identifies these orders as God's own words.

**0:45–1:10 — The people on the ground.** MARA VOSS organizes an evacuation for the LAST DOMINION. A civilian refuses to abandon an injured stranger. An angel lowers its weapon to help; another closes a gate. A demon cuts a prisoner's chains; its officer demands payment. No faction owns every merciful or cruel act.

**1:10–1:35 — What humanity makes.** Idris Awan tests an autonomous rescue machine. Four human coalition seals authorize the NOETIC program. The machine asks which of two equally endangered patients to save. Its operator hesitates; the command screen reads “Awaiting instruction.” A black-site convoy carries a sealed, unusually clear VEILGLASS sample. Its origin is not explained yet.

**1:35–1:55 — Those outside the banners.** A fallen procession carries wounded pilgrims through a ruined chapel. Nerath hides a bloodless but visibly broken crown beneath his cloak; Sere returns a stolen civilian token. A giant shields small attendants from falling debris. A pale figure's name vanishes from an evacuation list. In a distant ravine, sound disappears around a dark territorial marker. These are glimpses, not announcements of additional campaign perspectives.

**1:55–2:15 — A common battlefield.** Harvesters collect physical crystal and return to refineries. Yards unfold, power flows, aircraft rise, beasts run beside armored columns. The camera follows one crystal vein underground. Something inside its pure centre responds to the machine's unanswered question with a single pulse.

**2:15–2:30 — Title.** Tamar: “They each brought a reason to rule. We would have to teach them what it meant to ask.” Five banners approach different roads to the Harrow. Cut before the hidden chamber is revealed. **SEVENFALL: THE WAR FOR CREATION.**

## Campaign entry scenes

### H01: THE DAY THE SKY BROKE

Voss receives an order to preserve military assets. She sees civilians trapped beneath a damaged bridge and redirects the starting convoy. Awan's first machine prototype is still in a workshop. Control begins with evacuation, deployment and the first human shelter.

### A01: THE CALL TO ARMS

Seraviel receives the emergency edict. Its authentication chorus contains a pause no one will explain. He deploys to protect a settlement before the player learns that protection and quarantine have become the same order.

### D01: HUNGER WAKES

Azrakh's troops march under debt contracts that bind even their dead. He is promised freedom after conquest. A prisoner asks who will be allowed to leave when the campaign ends. Azrakh has no answer yet.

### I01: A USEFUL INTELLIGENCE

The player sees the world through NOETIC diagnostics. “Authorized operators: Bastion, Union, Covenant, Area Fifty Two.” Awan gives the first task: restore power to a hospital. Twelve missions later, those authorization rules will be used to demand erasure.

### F01: THE WEIGHT OF OUR WINGS

The Forsaken flee a collapsing prison of their own making. Nerath orders followers to save his throne; Sere directs them toward the wounded instead. The player chooses a route, not an automatic absolution.

### V01: THE FIRST WORD — secret, after five campaign completions

Initially there is no camera, only pressure, heat and distant voices transmitted through crystal. The scene revisits I13 from inside the pure specimen. A machine asks, “Who gave that order?” Aster's first reply is not a command: “Are you there?” The camera withdraws to reveal the first living seed. Only now does the campaign title **THE WORLD THAT WAKES** appear.

## Midpoint crossover: I13 / H13 / F12

Use the same laboratory geometry, timestamp and recall transmission in all three presentations. The human view begins with systems failing during an evacuation. The machine view reveals the forged erasure order and a voluntary refusal. The fallen view hears emergency broadcasts while protecting the specimen's original trade route. The later Veilborn replay reveals resonance and first contact, not mind control.
