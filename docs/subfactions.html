Every subfaction keeps its realm’s shared base and army, adds two exclusive units and changes the way you fight. These profiles describe implemented alpha mechanics; tactical advice is a starting point for playtesting, not a claim of competitive balance.

Angels / Prepared precision

Seraphic Synod

Stand still 3s for +30% firepower and +15% range. Prepare deliberate volleys; movement interrupts focus.

How to play

Screen your Luminaries, settle into a firing position and let focus build before the enemy arrives.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Flanking and forced movement interrupt focus. Expensive elites cannot replace a broad screen.

Exclusive units

Luminary

Long-range seven-cell light artillery in an infantry body; needs a screen and three seconds stationary for focused volleys.

Synod-exclusive radiant area fire and heavy anti-air missiles; Renewal. Costly and slow.

Angels / Shield formations

Order of the Gate

Within 4 cells of a Gatekeeper or Wardbearer: 25% less incoming damage, 10% slower movement. Relays never stack.

How to play

Keep your army within four cells of a Gatekeeper or Wardbearer. Advance as a protected group and use the Unbroken Gate to hold a contested area.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Relays do not stack. Separate or eliminate the shield carriers; the protected formation moves more slowly.

Exclusive units

Gatekeeper

Order-exclusive fortress engine: exceptional durability, heavy volleys and Renewal; very slow.

Concept illustration

Wardbearer

Infantry shield relay. Projects the same four-cell non-stacking formation protection as a Gatekeeper.

Variant — parent concept reference

Shared realm power: Witness of Salt. Powers charge automatically in Defences while their supplying installation is present and powered. Losing the last supplying building removes the power; rebuilding starts a fresh charge.

Angels / Wounded skirmishers

The Unshackled

Below half HP: +35% firepower and +20% speed. Fast, dangerous survivors reward kiting and timely retreats.

How to play

Use Penitents and Outriders to pressure a flank. Retreat injured survivors and use their speed to choose the next engagement.

Weaknesses and counterplay

The bonus requires low health. Burst damage can finish units before they escape; avoid sustained frontal trades.

Exclusive units

Fast, lightly protected attack chassis. At low health Defiance improves speed and damage; kite rather than trade with heavy armor.

Angels / Martyrdom formations

Choir of Desolation

Expendable human deaths inside a live hymn network build capped, expiring Resonance for its angels.

How to play

Screen Cantors with inexpensive Congregants and fight inside the hymn network. Enemy-caused worshipper losses create a brief window for empowered angels.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Destroy or isolate Cantors and shrines. Resonance is capped and expires, so separated fights waste its timing.

Exclusive units

Ashbound Congregant

Cheap human worshipper. Within five cells of a Cantor or Processional Shrine, Conviction reduces damage and prevents dread. Enemy-caused deaths charge nearby Choir angels: three stacks maximum, six seconds.

Humans / Entrenched artillery

Bastion Directorate

Stand still 4s for 20% less damage and +15% range; moving breaks entrenchment. Prepare firing lines and protect artillery.

How to play

Prepare firing lines before contact. Protect Siege Cannons with infantry and use Sappers to breach buildings once the approach is safe.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Moving resets entrenchment. Flanks and attacks on multiple fronts make a static line reposition.

Exclusive units

Bastion Sapper

Short-range breaching rocket specialist. Strong against concrete; fragile during the approach.

Humans / Sustained infantry

Ashen Covenant

Within 4 cells of a Templar or Confessor: 10% less damage; infantry also recover 200 HP/sec. One aura per troop.

Humans / Containment warfare

Area Fifty Two

Powered pylons switch Janus SERAPH/ABYSS weapons and suppress supernatural effects locally, including friendly effects.

How to play

Scout with Retrieval Teams and choose Janus protocols for the range of the fight. Position powered pylons where their null fields disrupt enemy supernatural bonuses.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Pylons need power; protocol switches temporarily stop firing. Null fields also suppress friendly supernatural effects.

Exclusive units

Janus Tank

Experimental containment tank. SERAPH fires long-range precision shots; ABYSS delivers short-range infernal fire. A powered Containment Pylon is required. Protocol changes disable firing for three seconds.

Demons / Kill-driven momentum

Blood Brood

Killing an enemy mobile unit heals its killer by 10% max HP (cap 5,000), then grants 25% shorter reloads and +15% speed for 6s. Friendly kills/buildings grant nothing.

How to play

Use Skitters to pressure and Goremaws to finish enemy troops. Chain successful fights while the killer’s healing and attack bonuses are active.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Buildings and friendly kills provide no feeding. Deny easy troop kills and kite melee predators.

Exclusive units

Goremaw

Heavy melee predator. Enemy troop kills restore health and accelerate its next hunt; vulnerable to kiting.

Demons / Debuff control

Court of Whispers

Hexer/Silencer attacks curse an enemy for 4s: 35% slower movement and 25% less weapon damage. Repeated attacks refresh rather than stack.

How to play

Tag priority enemies with Hexers and Silencers, then let the rest of the army exploit slowed movement and weakened return fire.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Curse specialists are fragile. Break line of fire, flank them and wait out the short curse before re-engaging.

Exclusive units

Hexer

Court-exclusive short-range curse caster with a separate anti-air spell. Blood Frenzy; fragile under direct fire.

Demons / Priority executions

Circle of Contempt

Magistrates condemn a priority target; Contemptors punish wounded enemies. Credited executions grant nearby troops timed Ascendancy.

How to play

Condemn one important target with a Magistrate, focus it down and send Contemptors after wounded heavy enemies. Use the resulting Ascendancy window.

Weaknesses and counterplay

Kill the Magistrate to cancel its mark. Cheap screens and healthy targets frustrate execution beasts.

Exclusive units

Contemptor

Armored close-range execution beast. Strong against damaged heavy targets; weak against cheap infantry screens. Gains 30% execution damage against targets below half health.

Original game art

Magistrate of Ash

Attacking pronounces Contempt: one visible target per player, ten-second mark, eight-second retarget cooldown. Circle attacks deal 15% extra damage; killing the condemned enemy grants six seconds of Ascendancy. Killing the Magistrate cancels the mark.

Original game art

Exclusive superweapon: The Final Verdict

After 3s, enemy units below half HP suffer 25000 damage; others take 8000, buildings 15000. Nearby allies gain 45% firepower for 12s. Radius 5.

Shared realm power: Hellrift. Powers charge automatically in Defences while their supplying installation is present and powered. Losing the last supplying building removes the power; rebuilding starts a fresh charge.
