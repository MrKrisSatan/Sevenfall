The Noetic Assembly and the Forsaken Host: two new realms, eight subfactions and sixteen exclusive units.

Alpha.3 expansion: five parent realms and twenty subfaction choices. 51 painted sprite sheets cover all current buildings and units in these realms, including MCVs and construction yards. Units have eight directions with idle, stride, action and wreck poses; buildings have six construction, operation and damage states. Additional animation polish remains planned. Damage values are before armor, doctrine and target modifiers. Competitive balance and broader Internet/full-match coverage remain playtest work.
