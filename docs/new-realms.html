The Noetic Assembly and the Forsaken Host: two new realms, eight subfactions and sixteen exclusive units.

Development expansion — not included in the published alpha.2 download. Five parent realms and twenty choices in development. 51 painted sprite sheets cover all current buildings and units in these realms, including MCVs and construction yards. Units have eight directions with idle, stride, action and wreck poses; buildings have six construction, operation and damage states. Additional animation polish remains planned. Damage values are before armor, doctrine and target modifiers. Competitive balance and expanded multiplayer acceptance remain pending.
