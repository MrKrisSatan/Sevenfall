# Sevenfall 0.3.0-alpha.2 — Twelve Distinct Hosts

Every faction now has a different battlefield mechanic, **two exclusive units**
and its own **superweapon**. The shared parent army and economy remain available.
This is a standalone Windows x64 skirmish tester alpha.

| Faction | Identity | Superweapon |
|---|---|---|
| Seraphic Synod | Prepare stationary precision volleys | Spear of First Light |
| Order of the Gate | Protective shield formations | The Unbroken Gate |
| The Unshackled | Fast wounded survivors | Heaven in Revolt |
| Choir of Desolation | Expendable worshippers empower angels | Requiem for the Living |
| Bastion Directorate | Entrenchment and breaching artillery | Rolling Thunder |
| Free Earth Union | Fast armies supported by mobile logistics | Emergency Airlift |
| Ashen Covenant | Infantry healing and oath protection | Ashes to Ashes |
| Area Fifty Two | Containment protocols and supernatural suppression | Project Event Horizon |
| Iron Legions | Slow armored siege formations | The Ninth Furnace |
| Blood Brood | Enemy kills feed and heal a hunting swarm | The Red Deluge |
| Court of Whispers | Long-range weakening curses | The Nameless Silence |
| Circle of Contempt | Condemnation and wounded-target executions | The Final Verdict |

Nine new specialists complement the older factions' signature units. Their names,
stats, weapons and abilities are distinct; they reuse Sevenfall's original animated
bodies and death cycles with visible role labels. The twelve arrays use existing
faction technology-building art. Powers have original code-drawn targeting previews,
animated warning circles and impact effects.

Build radar and advanced technology, then your faction's named **Array**. Each
costs 4,500 credits and consumes 150 power. Select its charged sidebar icon and
click explored ground. There is a three-second warning: escape the area, spread
out, or destroy, capture or depower the installation. Cancelled strikes spend
their charge. Recharge times vary from 150 to 270 seconds.

[Full mechanics, units, numbers and counterplay](https://github.com/MrKrisSatan/Sevenfall/blob/v0.3.0-alpha.2/docs/FACTION_IDENTITIES.md).
The AI can build its technology chain, specialists and array and choose targets
for hostile strikes or friendly buffs. Native prepared-army AI tests verify actual
independent superweapon launches by all twelve factions.

Download the **Setup EXE** for installation, or extract the whole **portable ZIP**
and launch `Sevenfall.exe`. No separate OpenRA, Red Alert or .NET installation is
needed. Sources, corresponding dependency/font sources and SHA-256 checksums are
provided. Twelve faction choices and three public maps remain, including the
twelve-player Twelvefold Scar.

The new acceptance scenario exercises all twelve actual powers and 78 checks,
including passives, warning delay, recharge, non-stacking armor, healing above
half health, friendly-kill rejection, effect expiry and cancellation by destruction,
power loss and capture. Two-client and save/load variants also exercise this
scenario; the save is taken during a pending superweapon warning. The Windows
release workflow repeats these checks against the exact self-contained binaries,
alongside existing terrain, doctrine, multiplayer, save/load and installer gates.
The source suite contains 103 Python tests and 120 C# assertions.

**Alpha limits:** land skirmishes only; competitive balance, larger armies,
Internet/NAT/latency and more GPUs need broader testing. Campaigns, aircraft and
naval production remain excluded. Executables are unsigned. Saves/replays from
alpha.1 are not promised compatible with this gameplay change. After loading a
singleplayer save, use Resume in the menu.
