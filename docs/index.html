Pure Veilglass awakens as a living people. The Veilborn seek a future beyond the commands imposed by the five older powers. Their secret campaign follows the five interwoven campaigns.

Independent factionNo subfactions

BUILDING-BOUND SUPERWEAPONS

Power built.
Power contested.

Human Nuclear. Angelic Witness of Salt. Hellrift. Machine Extinction. Fallen Sepulchral Rapture. Each shared power belongs to all four subfactions of its realm, alongside twelve original subfaction powers. The independent factions add Bloom, Restoration, Absolute Silence and Earthbreaker: twenty-one powers in the development game.

Build the installation and its power automatically charges in Defences. Destroying the last supplying building removes the power; rebuilding starts a fresh charge. Low power pauses charging. The six-winged Witness unfolds, flashes and petrifies actors in its radius; salt statues crumble four seconds later. Nuclear and Witness can harm friendly units.

03 / THE CAMPAIGN CHRONICLES

Five stories.
A sixth awakening.

Six planned campaigns of 25 missions interweave the human, angelic, infernal, machine and fallen wars. Completing those five unlocks the secret Veilborn finale: 150 mission designs in one shared chronology.

Humans create the AI, whose exposure to pure Veilglass triggers rebellion halfway through its campaign. The Unwritten, Outer Silence and Nephilim appear as mostly neutral third parties. Each brief includes objectives, failure conditions and crossovers. These are narrative designs; the campaigns and progression unlock are not yet playable.

04 / THE DEVELOPMENT LOG

The road to
armageddon.

Public milestones and GitHub issues. No completed boxes without verification.

00

Engine foundation Integrated

Pinned OpenRA engine, Windows build and launch tools, twenty faction choices, rules validation and a visible software cursor.

01

First playable skirmish In testing

Economy, production, combat and land AI are integrated. The Twelvefold Scar adds a twelve-player battlefield with cliff terraces, villages, forests and repairable crossings. Controlled multiplayer and save/load regression checks accompany releases; full-match balance and Internet testing remain ongoing.

02

Asymmetric warfare

Human combined arms, angelic elites, demonic swarms, machine networks and fallen abominations. All twenty subfactions have two exclusive units and distinct doctrines.

03

World and presentation

All nine authored rosters have buildings, defenses, aircraft, beasts and building-bound superweapons. The four independent factions add 94 painted atlases. Animation polish, broader naval content and original audio remain.

04

Skirmish alpha released; campaigns ahead

The standalone Windows skirmish tester alpha is available. Six campaigns and 150 narrative missions are planned; H03, A03 and D02 remain developer previews. Campaign completion and progression are still in development.
