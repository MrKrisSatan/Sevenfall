Explore Humans, Angels, Hell, the AI Noetic Assembly and the fallen Forsaken Host. All realms have buildings, defenses, aircraft, beasts and shared superweapons. Each new subfaction adds two exclusive units and a distinct doctrine. Six naval and fixed-wing production concepts are labelled as unavailable.
